Questions

Is it really free?

Yes. CrowdFax is a non-profit project. Ads on this page and donations pay for the faxes.

Do I need an account?

No. Upload a PDF, enter the recipient's fax number, and send.

Is my fax private?

The connection to this site is encrypted, and we plan to never store your documents. Faxes still pass through our fax provider and the phone network, which aren't end-to-end encrypted, so please don't send highly sensitive material.

When can I actually send one?

Sending is not switched on yet. We're in early access while we set up the fax service.
